I am a third-year game design student based in China. I enjoy turning curious ideas into playable prototypes — especially games about exploration, connection, and a little bit of surprise.

My current toolkit includes Unity, Unreal Engine, Figma, Blender, and a notebook full of sketches. Outside the screen, I collect tiny observations from everyday life and turn them into game ideas.
