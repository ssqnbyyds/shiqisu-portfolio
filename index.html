01 / CARD GAME · 1–4 PLAYERS

Pizza Cat
Board Game

A card-based tabletop game for 1–4 players, inspired by the clear, collectible language of Pokémon card design. Combine ingredient cards to make pizzas — for example, pineapple and ham for a Hawaiian pizza.

Each pizza earns points based on its ingredient combination. When the timer ends or the deck runs out, the player with the highest-scoring pizza collection wins.
